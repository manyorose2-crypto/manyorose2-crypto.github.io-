My Story

My name is Manyo Rose Eba, and I am 15 years old from ikom-okuni. I am a young girl who is passionate about learning and discovering new things. Through the AI for Her program, I am learning about technology, artificial intelligence, and digital skills. At first, technology felt difficult and unfamiliar to me, but I am becoming more confident as I learn.

As a young African girl, learning technology has not always been easy. Many girls face challenges such as limited access to devices, internet, and opportunities to learn digital skills. Sometimes, these challenges can make girls feel that technology is not meant for them. I am learning to break that mindset by giving myself the opportunity to learn, practice, make mistakes, and keep trying.

My dream is to use technology to make a positive difference in my community. I want to continue learning about artificial intelligence, programming, and other digital skills so that I can create solutions to real problems. I also want to encourage other girls to believe in themselves and explore technology. My goal is not just to learn technology, but to use what I learn to help others and create a better future.
